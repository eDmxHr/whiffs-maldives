# Vision, Mission, and Product Principles

## Vision

> To become the Maldives' most trusted destination for discovering and experiencing authentic fragrances.

## Mission

> Help people discover fragrances they truly love through authentic decants, curated experiences, and expert guidance.

## Long-Term Business Direction

The business is not intended to remain a decant-only shop. It is a fragrance discovery and commerce platform that launches with decants and is designed to scale into:

- Full bottle sales
- Gift boxes and gift collections
- Discovery sets and sample bundles
- Fragrance accessories
- Personalized recommendations
- Loyalty and subscription offerings
- Rich fragrance education and editorial content

The decant is the entry point. The long-term relationship is discovery, confidence, and eventual ownership.

## Product Positioning

The business serves beginners, collectors, students, office workers, gift buyers, and fragrance explorers across the Maldives.

The catalog is not limited to luxury fragrances. It can include:

- Middle Eastern brands
- Designer fragrances
- Niche fragrances
- Luxury fragrances
- Indie and artisan fragrances

## Product North Star

> Build the Maldives' most trusted fragrance discovery platform, where anyone—from a beginner exploring their first fragrance to a seasoned collector searching for their next favorite—can discover authentic fragrances with confidence through premium experiences, thoughtful guidance, and exceptional service.

## Product Principles

### Discovery Before Purchase

Every feature should help customers discover fragrances with confidence before committing to a full bottle.

### Authenticity Without Compromise

Every fragrance sold must be authentic and sourced from trusted sellers.

### Premium in Every Detail

The experience should feel refined from browsing through fulfillment and unboxing.

### Simple for Beginners, Powerful for Enthusiasts

Beginners should never feel intimidated, while enthusiasts should still receive rich, structured information.

### Build for Tomorrow

The architecture must support future full bottle sales and additional offering types without major redesign.

### Quality Over Quantity

A smaller catalog with complete metadata is preferred over a large but incomplete catalog.

### Operational Simplicity

When two approaches produce the same customer outcome, choose the easier one to operate and maintain.

### Automation Should Remove Work

Automation should support the business workflow instead of forcing unnecessary processes.

### One Source of Truth

Each important piece of information must have one authoritative owner.
