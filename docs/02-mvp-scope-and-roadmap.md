# MVP Scope and Roadmap

## MVP Goal

Launch a premium fragrance commerce platform that allows customers to discover authentic fragrances, purchase decants, and receive orders while enabling the owners to manage catalog, inventory, pricing, orders, payments, and fulfillment without spreadsheets.

## MVP Customer Capabilities

- Browse fragrances
- Search and filter fragrances
- View rich fragrance pages
- View brands and curated collections
- Choose 2 ml, 5 ml, or 10 ml decants
- Add offerings to cart
- Checkout as a guest
- Register and manage an account
- Use saved addresses
- Pay by bank transfer or cash on delivery
- View order history and tracking

## MVP Admin Capabilities

- Dashboard with actionable business insights
- Inbox for items requiring attention
- Fragrance and brand management
- Rich fragrance metadata management
- Image upload and ordering
- Bottle inventory management
- FIFO bottle usage
- Offering and pricing management
- Packaging profiles and packaging inventory
- Customer management
- Order and payment management
- Partial fulfillment
- Inventory adjustments and bottle ledger
- Sales, order, bottle, and profitability analytics

## Excluded from Initial MVP

- Full bottle sales
- Reviews and community features
- AI recommendation engine
- Loyalty and referrals
- Subscription boxes
- Automated courier integrations
- Automatic label printing
- Social login
- Mobile application

The architecture must still support these additions later.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Validate

- Decants
- Rich fragrance catalog
- Orders
- Manual payment confirmation
- Bottle inventory
- Fulfillment
- Premium packaging

### Phase 2 — Optimize

- Better analytics
- Wishlist
- Customer notifications
- Improved recommendations
- Purchase history insights

### Phase 3 — Expand

- Full bottles
- Gift sets
- Discovery sets
- Accessories
- Loyalty and referrals

### Phase 4 — Intelligence

- Personalized recommendations
- Fragrance profiles
- Similarity and note-based matching
- Layering suggestions

### Phase 5 — Community

- Reviews
- User shelves
- Collections
- Fragrance journal
