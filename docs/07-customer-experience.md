# Customer Experience

## Experience Goal

The website should feel like a modern fragrance boutique and discovery platform rather than a generic online shop.

## Homepage

Core sections:

- Single focused hero
- Browse fragrances CTA
- Why decants
- Browse by experience, occasion, note, brand, budget, and collection
- Featured collections
- Best sellers
- New arrivals
- How it works
- Authenticity and trust
- Packaging experience
- Newsletter or new-arrival signup later

## Browse Experience

Customers should be able to discover through:

- Search
- Brand
- Notes
- Accords
- Fragrance family
- Occasion
- Season
- Maldives climate suitability
- Price
- Decant size
- Availability
- New arrivals and best sellers

## Fragrance Page

The page should educate before it sells.

Core sections:

- Image gallery
- Brand, name, concentration, and summary
- Editorial introduction
- Fragrance character
- Main accords
- Full note pyramid
- Performance
- Climate and occasions
- Who it suits
- Decant and future full bottle offerings
- Price and availability
- Authenticity message
- Similar fragrances
- Should I buy this summary

## Offering Selector

Initial offering options:

- 2 ml — first impression
- 5 ml — several wears
- 10 ml — longer experience

Unavailable sizes remain visible as out of stock.

## Checkout

- Guest checkout supported
- Account registration encouraged but not required
- Saved addresses for registered users
- Male' and island delivery details
- Bank transfer and cash on delivery
- Clear payment and stock confirmation messaging
